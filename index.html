Our Story

Turning Ambition Into Opportunity.

Cranberry Overseas Education Consultants was founded in 2009 with a single purpose: to make world-class international education accessible to every student, regardless of background or financial situation.

We treat each student's education as the prime foundation of their career. Our expert counsellors help you identify the right course, the right university, and the right country — tailored precisely to your academic goals and personal aspirations.

Today, we operate across Thane, Dadar, Nashik, Dombivli, and Vashi — guiding students to life-changing opportunities in the UK, USA, Australia, Canada, Ireland, New Zealand, Singapore, Dubai, and Europe.

Promoting Global Education.

To promote overseas education through partnerships with leading universities in the USA, UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, Ireland, and Europe — opening doors to transformative academic experiences for every Indian student.
